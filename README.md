# 📊 Customer Churn & Sales Analysis

### Turning Customer Data into Meaningful Business Insights 📈

An interactive **Business Analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI and Excel** to analyze sales performance, understand customer behavior, and explore the factors associated with customer churn.

The project analyzes **500 customer records** and transforms raw business data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards, visualizations, and key performance indicators (KPIs).

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboards and data visualization
* **Microsoft Excel** — Dataset management and analysis
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** — Calculating business metrics and KPIs

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing patterns, sales performance, and customer churn to support better business decisions.

The project focuses on identifying sales trends, comparing customer segments, understanding satisfaction levels, and exploring possible customer retention challenges.

## 📋 Dataset Features

The dataset consists of **500 unique customer records and 19 columns**, covering customer information, sales transactions, purchasing behavior, and customer churn.

**👥 Customer Information**

* Customer ID and Customer Name
* Gender and Age
* City
* Customer Type (New or Returning)

**🛒 Sales Information**

* Product and Category
* Sales and Quantity
* Order Date
* Payment Mode
* Discount Percentage
* Total Orders

**📉 Customer Behavior & Churn**

* Last Purchase Date
* Customer Tenure (Months)
* Support Calls
* Satisfaction Score
* Churn Status (Yes/No)

## 💡 Key Insights & Findings

The analysis of 500 customer records provided several useful observations.

**📌 1. Customer Retention**

Out of 500 customers, 122 have churned, resulting in an overall churn rate of **24.4%**. This means approximately one in four customers in the dataset is marked as churned.

**📌 2. Sales Performance**

The business recorded total sales of **4,493,600** across 5,135 total orders. Snacks emerged as the highest-selling product, generating 304,567 in sales.

**📌 3. Product Demand**

Skin Care Kit recorded the highest aggregated number of orders, with **355 orders**, followed by Snacks with 348.

**📌 4. City-Wise Performance**

Kanpur recorded the highest sales contribution, generating **565,950**, making it the leading city in the dataset.

**📌 5. Customer Type & Churn**

Among the 122 churned customers:

* 66 were New customers.
* 56 were Returning customers.

The churn rate was 24.8% for New customers and 23.9% for Returning customers.

**📌 6. Gender-Based Churn**

Both male and female customer groups recorded 61 churned customers each. However, their churn rates differed:

* Female customers: 22.3%
* Male customers: 26.9%

**📌 7. Satisfaction & Churn**

The overall average satisfaction score was **3.02 out of 5**.

Customers with a satisfaction score of 2 recorded 35 churned customers out of 109, giving a churn rate of approximately 32.1%.

**📌 8. Customer Support Interactions**

Customers making 7 support calls recorded 17 churn cases out of 53 customers, while customers making 8 calls recorded 16 churn cases out of 50.

These patterns highlight customer groups that may require further analysis.
